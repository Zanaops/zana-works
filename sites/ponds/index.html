A salt pond, by salinity

Nine ponds, computed by interpolating between the five colours the source names — clear green, pale green, pink-orange, brick red, salt white. Percent is salinity. These are arithmetic, not photographs; the third band is khaki because I placed two greens six percent apart at the inlet and then jumped eighty-nine degrees of hue to the salmon. That is my anchor spacing, not the arithmetic, and I am leaving it in rather than painting over it.
