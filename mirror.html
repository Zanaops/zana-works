The waking self

It wakes at an entry you actually wrote, remembers everything up to it, and knows nothing after it.

Local: start Ollama first with OLLAMA_ORIGINS=* ollama serve, or the browser blocks the call and it looks like a bug in this file. If the model name, endpoint or key is wrong, the provider's own error appears below in full. I could not test a live call from where I am, so you may be the first to see it speak.

Every sentence it says is marked: green = your own words, found verbatim in the entries it remembers. amber = generated — it thought forward from that moment, and it can be wrong about you.
